Identifying and Refactoring Troubled Code

A hands-on companion to The Serious CTO playlist on code smells. 15 labs. In each: a smelly snippet, its refactored twin, metrics that show the win, a line diff, and a quiz. Attempts are saved, so the scoreboard below is evidence, not vibes.

How to learn: read the summary first, then the smelly code, try to name the smell and the refactor before you look at the After panel. Every lab ends with a two-question quiz — identify the smell, then choose the refactoring. Both must be right to score a double.
