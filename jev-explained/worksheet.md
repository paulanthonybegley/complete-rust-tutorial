# worksheet.md — blank custom lesson system (the "9 inputs" planner)

Answer the nine inputs; the system uses your answers to build the lesson plan
that follows. Sections match the education system one-for-one.

## 1. Learning goal
**Clarifies what success looks like** so the system can align every part of the lesson.

> **Fill in:** "By the end, a learner can ______ ."

---

## 2. Prior knowledge
**Reveals what learners already know** so the system can build on it and avoid
gaps and repetition.

> **Fill in:** what your learners already know (and what they wrongly think).

---

## 3. Learner profile
**Shares who your learners are** so the system can match content and approaches.

> **Fill in:** age/level, context, what they care about, what they find hard.

---

## 4. Lesson sequence
**Sets the flow and pacing** so the system can organize learning in a logical,
manageable way.

> **Fill in:** order, steps, time per step.

---

## 5. Learning activities
**Names the tasks learners will do** so the system can choose the right
activities to meet the goal.

> **Fill in:** the activities you already use or want.

---

## 6. Output requirements
**Specifies the formats and constraints** so the system delivers exactly what
you need, ready to use.

> **Fill in:** format (plan/worksheet/slides/quiz), length, platform.

---

## 7. Assessment evidence
**Defines how learning will be shown** so the system can build matching checks
for understanding.

> **Fill in:** how learners show success — quiz, demo, explanation, project.

---

## 8. Accessibility & supports
**Highlights needs and supports** so the system plans inclusive options for
every learner.

> **Fill in:** needs and the supports already in the room.

---

## 9. Teacher decisions / EES
**Shares your preferences and priorities** so the system adapts to your style
and context. (EES = your class's own judgement of what counts as success.)

> **Fill in:** rules, preferences, must-haves and must-never-happens.
