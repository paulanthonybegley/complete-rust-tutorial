# LinkedIn Post: European Independence Course

---

**🇪🇺 What does "European independence" actually mean? I turned a State of the Union speech into an interactive learning lab.**

Every September the Commission president gives the State of the Union speech. This year's was about one idea: *independence* — from volatile powers, from fragile grids, from single-source materials.

I turned the analysis video into a hands-on Spring Boot 4 course. Nine themes, nine lessons, one interactive app.

**1️⃣ The speech itself** — "strongest it has ever been" and "precarious" at the same time. Decoupling from Russia, China and the US.

**2️⃣ Economic resilience = speed** — faster permitting, single market by 2027, the 16% of corporate lending that crosses borders, omnibus simplification (€17bn/yr). "Pact against gold-plating."

**3️⃣ Critical raw materials** — ~90% of EU imports from China in places. The plan: joint procurement + strategic stockpiles.

**4️⃣ Defence & security** — €1.5bn defence industry programme, €90bn joint debt, an EU Article 4, a counter-hybrid playbook, a European Security Council, strategic enablers.

**5️⃣ Energy** — double electricity's share by 2040, cut the fossil import bill by ~€260bn/yr. 80 GW of renewables added last year; 6× that waiting on a grid connection.

**6️⃣ Climate** — Europe warms 2× faster than the world. Insurance alliance, heatwave plans, drought strategies, a firefighting fleet.

**7️⃣ AI sovereignty** — European compute, funded scale-ups, model-safety cooperation, industrial AI in 5 sectors (health, transport, agri-food, advanced manufacturing, defence & space).

**8️⃣ Partnerships** — Canada as first "associate member", trade deals with India and Mercosur, the €12bn Middle Corridor, and a €1bn-a-day China trade deficit to fix.

**9️⃣ Media literacy** — 157 sources, left and right framing of the *same* announcement. Bias-awareness as a lesson, not a lecture.

---

**Why I built it differently:** the app is a dashboard + policy explorer + 3 simulators + self-grading quiz + flip cards — all server-rendered with **Thymeleaf and htmx**, no frontend build, no database. Students *move* the numbers (China-dependency slider, electricity-share target, Security Council builder) and the score changes live.

"Make me a worksheet" → "build a system for your class." The lesson-plan framework in the repo makes every lesson repeatable: goal, sequence, evidence, profile, prior knowledge, activities, outputs, accessibility, teacher decisions.

The whole course ships as runnable Spring Boot 4 code. Build it, boot it, hand it to a classroom.
