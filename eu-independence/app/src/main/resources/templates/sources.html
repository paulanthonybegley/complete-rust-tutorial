📋 Before you share — five checks

  1. Source & date — who published, and when? A week-old claim about a speech is different from today's.
  2. Primary text — go to the actual speech transcript, not a summary of a summary.
  3. Two frames — read one left-leaning and one right-leaning account of the same story and list what each emphasises.
  4. Ownership — an outlet's owner and funding tell you which interests it serves.
  5. The figure — confirm the statistic is quoted (90%, 16%, €260bn, 157…) against a primary or named source.
