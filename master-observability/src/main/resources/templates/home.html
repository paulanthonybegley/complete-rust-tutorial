Mastering Observability Engineering

Everything from The Serious CTO's "Mastering Observability Engineering Made Easy!" playlist, built as one mildly broken shop you can drive with your own hands. The two ideas that keep showing up in every lab: observability has three pillars — logs, metrics, traces — and they are only worth anything when they are correlated. This app links every pillar by a single trace id, so you can chase a failed order from dashboard → trace → log line → metric, end to end.

01 · Link the three

One checkout. One trace id appearing in a log line, on a span, and next to the checkout metric. The keystone of the whole course.

02 · Structured logs

JSON out of logback, trace ids in the log line, Loki storing it all and answering queries like the dashboards do.

03 · Business metrics

Prometheus buckets on latency, outcomes and failure reasons. Generate your own traffic and read the numbers it produces.

04 · Traces & spans

Every checkout is a root span with children. Open any trace in the app or hand it over to the Jaeger UI.

05 · Correlate end-to-end

The debug procedure the videos teach, top-down: failing order → its trace → the error on a span → its logs → the metric move.

06 · Sample smartly

At scale you keep only what matters. Compare the app-side keep/keep-else and the collector's tail_sampling on one page.

07 · Alert, not fatigue

Real Prometheus rules that fire only on business impact, Alertmanager receipts sat in the database, and the fatigue warning itself.

08 · Hygiene

Two identical log calls, redacted line vs leaked line, side by side in Loki. Prove PII masking to yourself.

09 · Incident drill

Capstone: fabricate an outage, solve it with the top-down procedure alone, and get a verdict on your diagnosis.

10 · At scale

Log aggregation and sampling pipelines, autoscaling off business metrics, and every target Prometheus is poking right now.

11 · Grafana

Provisioned dashboards of the shop: checkout SLO, success/failure rates, latency, sampling volume, alert state.
