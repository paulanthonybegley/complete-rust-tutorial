why mask at the call site, not "in the pipeline"?

The regexes live in obs/SafeLog: emails, 13–16 digit card numbers, and password= / token= / secret= style key-values become [redacted]. But the interesting part of this lab is ordering: the redaction runs in the application, before the message reaches logback. A JSON encoder, a collector, Loki — none of them can reliably un-leak a field a developer already logged raw, and no sanitizing pipeline can be trusted to catch every freeform string. So the rule that actually protects data is the boring one: never put PII in a log call to begin with; when you must, mask it where it enters. The leak column is what "someone will remember to mask later" costs when someone doesn't.
