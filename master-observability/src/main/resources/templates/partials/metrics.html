histogram_quantile(0.95, …) — why this shape?

Prometheus buckets are counters: …_bucket{le="…"} count observations up to each bound. The histogram is approximate — a percentile is recomputed out of the bucket sums, so it is only as fine as the bucket layout. That's why the app enables percentiles-histogram on the checkout timer: it asks Micrometer/Prometheus to emit finer bucket boundaries automatically.
