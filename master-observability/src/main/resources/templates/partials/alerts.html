No webhooks yet. The full loop is: Prometheus rule evaluates → fires → Alertmanager receives it → notifies the webhook → this app inserts a row. ~15 failing checkouts over 30s usually trips it. Patience: the alert needs a 5m lookback to compute the ratio and 30s to go from pending → firing.

why is "alert fatigue" a design decision, not a complaint?

Every alert you add costs attention; attention is the only resource a team never buys more of. The videos' test for a good alert: what action will the on-call engineer take when it fires? If the answer is "restart something / ignore it", the alert should probably be a dashboard instead. ShopInstanceDown exists because an SLO like "checkouts under 1s" goes silent at 00:00 — a down target alerts you that the ping itself broke.
