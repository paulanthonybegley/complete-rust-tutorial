why do I only see fields on the left of {selector}?

Loki indexes labels — the key-value pairs inside the selector braces. The full JSON line is the log stream, stored once per unique label set. LogQL filters like |= scan the stream, but only labels are indexed. That is why promtail promotes level and trace_id into labels (see promtail-config.yml) — those are what make {service="app",level="ERROR"} cheap, and why "grep everything" hits correlate+alerts+hook-metric hard.
