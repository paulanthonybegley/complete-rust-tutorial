Autoscaling on a business metric — the pattern

The plain-CPU-autoscale is what the playlist mocks: CPU pegs after load, then recovery lags. Here the control loop instead reads the checkout queue directly:

  1. Traffic lands in a queue, not on a fixed pool.
  2. Gauge checkout_queue_depth (exposed by the app's Micrometer) is scraped every 10s by Prometheus.
  3. A recorder rule derives the scheduling target: ceil(queue / (workers_sec · max_queue_each)) — add pods when the queue backs up, when failures spike, or when latency p95 crosses its budget.
  4. Kubernetes HPA (or any autoscaler) just scales that one series. Same math you already read on /metrics and the dashboards.

The point: whether it is a page, a queue, or a fleet — you can only cut over on a metric you already trust. Every lab here built that trust.
