PII masking — prove it end to end

The videos list hygiene rules: never log secrets or PII, mask card numbers and emails, and know what your log pipeline does with them. This app logs a "customer signup" line two ways — safe, where the message passes through obs.SafeLog and gets its email and password redacted before the line leaves the JVM, and leak, where the same data is logged raw — the mistake that causes real incidents.
