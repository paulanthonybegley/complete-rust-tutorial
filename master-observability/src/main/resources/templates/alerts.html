LAB 07 · ALERTS, NOT FATIGUE

Rules that fire on business impact

The playlist is explicit about alert fatigue: alert when it costs you, not on every blip. Two rules are provisioned in prometheus/alert-rules.yml — FailedCheckoutRateHigh (failure ratio > 10% for 30s) and ShopInstanceDown. When one fires, Alertmanager posts a webhook; this app stores the receipt in the database, and the table below polls both sources every 5s.

To make it fire: POST stress below (or /metrics "fail hard") and keep the ratio over 10% for ~30 seconds. ~15 of the last 20 checkouts failing does exactly that.
