LAB 06 · OBSERVABILITY AT SCALE

Sampling: keep the failures, skip the chaff

When you do a million requests a minute you cannot store a million traces. The videos are explicit: sample intelligently — keep everything that is broken, slow, or paying, and drop the rest. Two layers decide here: the app-side TraceDecider (primary decision, stored in the database) and the Collector's tail_sampling policy (what actually lands in Jaeger). This lab fires a burst of synthetic requests and lets you compare the two layers.

Policy on the collector (otel-collector-config.yml): error → keep, slow ≥ 300ms → keep, user.tier=vip → keep, else random 40%.
