When NOT to use Postgres

"Just use Postgres" is powerful, but it is not a silver bullet. The video ends with a warning: don't abandon your critical thinking.

Workload Why Postgres falls short Reach for
Reading millions of events / second One primary node becomes a bottleneck ClickHouse, Kafka + stream processing
Sub-millisecond in-memory caching at huge concurrency RAM cache in Postgres is very fast but not that fast Redis / Memcached
Extreme horizontal scale Postgres scales vertically beautifully; sharding mono DB is painful Distributed systems (CockroachDB, TiDB, ...)
Billion-row + analytics at warehouse speed A materialized view is not a decoupled columnar warehouse Snowflake / ClickHouse

The verdict from the video

Until you cross that threshold of massive enterprise scale, leaning on the core battle-tested mechanics of Postgres to run your entire stack is arguably the smartest and most cost-effective engineering decision you can make.
