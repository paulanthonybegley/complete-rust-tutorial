Network Policies: traffic by permission

By default Kubernetes allows everything between pods. A NetworkPolicy opts pods in to rules: pick the target with a selector, choose Ingress (who may send in), Egress (who may reach out), then list the sources/destinations allowed. The moment a selected pod gets no rule, its traffic is denied — that's the famous default-deny.
