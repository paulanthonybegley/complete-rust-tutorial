Inside the black box: kubectl exec & cp

Sometimes you must get into the container — read a config file, inspect env, copy a file in for debugging. The video shows the mental model: exec runs one process inside the container; cp shuffles files between your machine and the pod's filesystem. Both, here, are simulated but honest: this pod's virtual filesystem is real inside this app.

Best practice from the video

exec/cp are for debugging, not solutioning: a fix you apply by hand evaporates the moment the pod dies. Treat containers as cattle; write the fix into the manifest or image instead.
