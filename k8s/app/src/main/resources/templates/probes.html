Startup, Liveness & Readiness probes

Three probe types, three jobs. startup protects a slow-booting container from being restarted; liveness restarts a container that is alive but useless; and readiness decides whether a healthy-but-busy pod should receive traffic. Flip a scenario and watch the three web pods react.

probequestion it answersaction on failure
startup"has the process finished booting?"keeps liveness/readiness paused
liveness"is the container still alive?"kubelet kills & restarts the container
readiness"is it safe to send this pod traffic?"removes the pod from Service endpoints

Notice the difference in the Restarts column: a liveness failure bumps the restart counter and the phase flips to CrashLoopBackOff; a readiness failure leaves the phase green but marks Ready false.
