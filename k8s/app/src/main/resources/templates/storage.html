Storage that survives the pod

Inside a container, writes are ephemeral; delete the pod and they're gone. The escape hatch is a PersistentVolume (storage that exists independently) claimed by a PersistentVolumeClaim (your request form), mounted into the pod at a path. This station proves the difference by writing files and bouncing the pod.

A claim is Pending until the scheduler matches it to a compatible PV (class, mode, size).

The lifecycle
  • Static: admin pre-creates PVs; claims bind to them.
  • Dynamic: a StorageClass with a provisioner makes PVs on demand.
  • Reclaim: Retain / Delete / Recycle decides what happens to data when the claim is released.

What you should have seen

Write a file to /data/ and to /data/persistent/. Now recreate the pod — both are gone from the pod view… but the persistent one is back, because a bound PVC outlives its consumer. emptyDir dies with the pod. That's the whole story.
