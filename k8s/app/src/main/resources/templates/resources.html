Requests, Limits & the QoS classes

Requests are what the scheduler books for you — they decide whether a pod fits a node. Limits are the cap the kubelet enforces: CPU over the limit gets throttled; memory over the limit gets an OOM kill. The relationship between them puts every pod into one of three QoS classes, which decides its eviction priority when the node is under pressure.

The three classes, from the video
  • BestEffort — no requests, no limits. First to be evicted, first to oversubscribe.
  • Burstable — some value set, or requests < limits. Guaranteed its request, may burst.
  • Guaranteed — requests equal limits on CPU and memory. Last to evict.
